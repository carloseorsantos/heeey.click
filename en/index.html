How do you use the Heeey online whiteboard?

Open a new board, share the link and everyone draws on the same screen, seeing each person's cursor live.

  1. 1

    Create a board

    Click new board and start drawing. You can try it without an account; your work is saved in the browser.

  2. 2

    Share the link

    Choose whether people with the link can edit or only watch, and send it to your class or team.

  3. 3

    Draw together

    Each person shows up with a name and color on their cursor. Changes reach everyone instantly.

What can you do on the whiteboard?

Everything you'd do on a real whiteboard, with Excalidraw's hand-drawn style and a few things paper can't do.

  • Shapes, arrows and text

    Rectangles, circles, arrows that snap to shapes, text and freehand drawing.

  • Real-time collaboration

    Named cursors, a laser pointer and each person's selection, visible to everyone.

  • Images

    Paste or drag images onto the board to draw notes over them.

  • Folders and search

    Organize boards in folders and find any of them by the text written inside.

  • Version history

    Go back to an earlier version of the board when someone deletes what they shouldn't have.

  • Element libraries

    Use ready-made libraries of icons and components to build diagrams faster.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Heeey online whiteboard free?

Yes. You can create boards, share them and collaborate in real time without paying anything.

Do I need an account to use it?

Not to try it: you can draw as a guest, and the board is saved in your browser. With an account, your boards live in the cloud and you get folders, search and version history. Boards you created as a guest move to your account when you sign in.

How do I sign in without a password?

You type your email and get a sign-in link (a magic link). Click it and you're in. There's no password to forget.

Can I use it to teach online?

Yes. Share the link as "View only" so students can follow along live, or as "Can edit" so they can solve exercises with you on the same board.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

It works in the browser on any device, with no app to install.

What happens if the internet goes down?

Nothing is lost. Every stroke is saved in your browser first and synced to the cloud when the connection comes back.
